Privacy policy

Last updated Nov. 20, 2026

This policy covers inboxsolved.com and the Inbox Solved email list. Inbox Solved is published by Kenyon Business Strategists LLC ("KBS," "we").

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect the email address you enter. Our email service, Kit, also records when you signed up and whether you open or click our emails.

This site sets no cookies. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies or local storage and does not track individual visitors. We see totals only, such as page views, which pages people read, and which sites sent them, never who you are.

What we do with your email address

  • Send you the Inbox Solved download link.
  • Send you occasional emails about Inbox Solved and related tools.

We never sell, rent, or trade your email address.

Unsubscribing and deleting your information

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click takes you off the list. To have your information deleted entirely, email us at the address below.

The Inbox Solved skill and your Gmail

The Inbox Solved skill runs inside your own Claude account and reaches your Gmail through Claude's Gmail connector, which you set up and control. KBS never sees, receives, or stores your email. Your use of Claude and Gmail is covered by the privacy policies of Anthropic and Google. You can disconnect Gmail from Claude at any time under Customize, then Connectors.
